Elevation and Vegetation Health Spatial Analysis of a Creswell, Oregon Hazelnut Orchard.

A group project completed in June 2026 by myself and 4 others for an upper division geography class at the University of Oregon.
My contribution: processed RGB, MS, and LiDAR data using Agisoft Metashape, ArcGIS Pro, and R (RStudio) to produce deliverables. Light editing of the report.
